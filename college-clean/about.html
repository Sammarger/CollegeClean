Our Story

Nottingham's Student Cleaning Specialists

College Clean was founded to solve a real problem — students losing deposits and landlords struggling to find reliable cleaners at scale. Too many properties were being left in a state of dispute, with tenants and landlords in conflict over standards that a good clean could have prevented.

We built a team around high standards, flexible scheduling and honest pricing. Every job is treated the same regardless of size — whether it's a single-bed studio or a ten-room HMO, the level of care doesn't change.

Our staff are DBS-checked and trained to landlord-ready standards, so property managers and letting agents can rely on us to work professionally without supervision. We also have experience in office and commercial cleaning for clients who need more than just accommodation covered.
